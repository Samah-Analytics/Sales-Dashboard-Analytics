# Case Study: Data-Driven Sales Optimization Strategy

## Business Overview & Challenges
This project analyzes the commercial operations of a multi-category retailer serving major urban markets (Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore). The business manages a diverse inventory across **Sports Equipment, Beauty Products, and Home Goods**.

### The Core Challenges
Despite achieving **$1.0M in Total Sales** with a highly efficient **67% Profit Rate ($914K Profit)** across **3K units**, critical friction points limit long-term scaling:
*   **Unmanaged Seasonality:** Sharp, recurring revenue drops in specific months create cash-flow bottlenecks.
*   **Stagnant Capital:** Too much inventory budget is tied up in slow-moving, low-margin products.
*   **Expansion Blindspots:** Leadership lacks localized performance data to open new sales channels safely.

---

## Strategic Project Goals
*   **Isolate Revenue Valleys:** Identify specific low-sales months to predict and mitigate demand slumps.
*   **Optimize Product Mix:** Identify low-profit items to phase out or replace with high-margin alternatives.
*   **Map Growth Pathways:** Cross-reference regional demand to open proven geographic sales channels.
*   **Inventory Playbook:** Design a "pre-cleanse" promotional plan to clear slow stock during seasonal dips.

---

## Workflow & Tech Stack

### 1. Data Prep (Microsoft Excel)
*   Cleaned raw transactional data (removed duplicates, handled null values, unified regional names).
*   Structured flat tables into an optimized format for relational modeling.

### 2. Modeling & Visualization (Power BI)
*   Built a star-schema data model linking sales dates, products, and locations.
*   Designed an interactive executive dashboard optimized for instant scannability.

## Strategic Recommendations

1.  **Deploy Promotional Bundles:** Run targeted clearance campaigns specifically right before the identified lowest sales months to clear inventory and maintain cash flow.
2.  **Pivot to Alternative Products:** Reallocate procurement budget away from non-performing items and invest heavily in high-margin categories like Sports and Beauty.
3.  **Expand Proven Channels:** Scale physical or digital footprints specifically into high-performing regions like Islamabad and Karachi where customer traction is already verified.
